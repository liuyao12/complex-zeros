Drag blue zeros or pink diamond poles. Add-zero and add-pole modes update the entire rational map under the pointer before you click. Drag empty space to pan; use the wheel to zoom. Critical points are constrained outputs: a single one cannot generally move while all the others and all poles stay fixed. “Move zeros (W fixed)” uses the exact family f+c. “Point → ∞” is a Möbius change of affine chart, not deletion of the point.

Finite critical points are roots of D=P′Q−PQ′. If the finite zero and pole counts differ, the missing divisor lies at ∞. The reciprocal chart is padded to the common map degree, so separatrices ending at or crossing ∞ are traced on the sphere.

For fixed poles, the admissible critical divisor satisfies residue constraints. By contrast, f↦f+c fixes every pole and every critical point while moving the zero divisor.
