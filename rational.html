The critical points are the zeros of D=P′Q−PQ′. The desingularized field ż=−P Q · conjugate(D) has the same unparametrized trajectories as ż=−f/f′.

Forward branches end at zeros and backward branches begin at poles. The colored curves are exactly the levels |f(z)|=|f(w)| through critical points w. The tracer changes to u=1/z when a spherical edge crosses infinity.
