Finite critical points are roots of D=P′Q−PQ′. If the finite zero and pole counts differ, the missing divisor lies at ∞. The reciprocal chart is padded to the common map degree, so separatrices ending at or crossing ∞ are traced on the sphere.

For each finite zero, the bar is the shortest resolved incident Newton-edge arc divided by the zero-disk radius R. The orange marker is the ordinary distance to the nearest finite critical point. The sharp universal rational theorem controls that straight distance by 2R; the curved graph length is a stronger experimental statistic.
