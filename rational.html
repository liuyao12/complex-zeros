Finite critical points are roots of D=P′Q−PQ′. If the finite zero and pole counts differ, the missing divisor lies at ∞. The reciprocal chart is padded to the common map degree, so separatrices ending at or crossing ∞ are traced on the sphere.

For a finite zero a, define r(a)=max |a−α| over all zeros α. The disk centered at a with radius r(a) contains the full zero fiber, so the rational circular-region theorem guarantees a critical point in it. The orange marker is that nearest-critical straight distance divided by r(a); the white bar is the stronger experimental Newton-edge arc length divided by r(a).
